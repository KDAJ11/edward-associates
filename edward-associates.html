Expert Tax Tips & Advice

Practical insights to help you save money and stay compliant year-round

01

Keep Digital Records

Scan and organize your receipts digitally throughout the year. CRA accepts digital records, and you'll save hours during tax season when everything is ready to go.

02

Maximize Your RRSP

Contributing to your RRSP before the March 1st deadline can reduce your taxable income significantly. Even small regular contributions add up to big savings over time.

03

Don't Miss Work-From-Home Deductions

If you worked from home in 2024, you may be eligible for home office expense deductions. Keep records of your workspace costs and employment conditions.

04

Track Medical Expenses

Medical expenses over 3% of your net income are deductible. This includes prescriptions, dental work, and even certain over-the-counter medications with a doctor's note.

05

File Even With No Income

Filing a tax return even when you have no income can help you receive benefit payments, build RRSP room, and maintain your GST/HST credits.

06

Small Business? Separate Your Finances

Keep personal and business expenses completely separate. A dedicated business account makes bookkeeping easier and protects you during CRA audits.
